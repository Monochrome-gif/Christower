Frequently asked questions

Quick answers for parents and guardians.

Which classes do you offer?

We offer nursery, primary and secondary education, including junior (JSS) and senior (SSS) secondary classes.

How do I apply for admission?

Fill in the enquiry form on this page, or call or message the school on WhatsApp. The school will guide you through the next steps.

Can I visit the school before applying?

Yes. Contact the school to arrange a visit and ask about any assessment for your child's class.

What are the fees and the next term's dates?

Fees and term dates can change, so please call or message the school for the current details.

How do students and parents stay in touch with the school?

Use the Connect page for student communication, or reach the school by phone, WhatsApp or email.
